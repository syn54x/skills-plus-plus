A `grilling` Round can now be answered with letters: every option sits on its own line as `A:`, `B:` and so on, the recommended one first, with a `Rests on:` line naming what the recommendation assumes, and a reply such as `1A 2B` or `all` answers the whole Round, with a caveat after any letter. Each Round after the first reads back the replies that changed the plan, says how many choices are settled and which are held back, and lists the facts it looked up with their sources. The closing restatement marks each line with its source (you, the record, or the agent's inference) and puts the inferred lines last as the ones to check. In `auto-grill`, the veto list stands in for the list of looked-up facts.
