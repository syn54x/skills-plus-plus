`/setup-spp` now asks two questions: the repo's Self-improvement mode (Off, Anonymous or Full Detail), written to the Routing block, and your own Submission mode (Auto or Review), stored for you with `spp feedback submission`. It recommends Full Detail and Auto for a public repo and Anonymous and Review for a private one. A block that still says `Upstream feedback:` has the old choice carried forward as a Self-improvement mode.
