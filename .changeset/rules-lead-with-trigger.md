`writing-for-agents` now tells authors how to word a rule an agent obeys while working (an `AGENTS.md` invariant, a `CLAUDE.md` law): lead with its trigger, the moment it applies, then the check that discharges it, and put the incident that named it last. One paragraph under "Steps and completion criteria" carries a before/after example, and the docs page gains the same point plus a Common questions entry. A rule written as a story with the rule at the end has no moment to fire at; a trigger-shaped one is what `implement-issue` workers check a law against.
