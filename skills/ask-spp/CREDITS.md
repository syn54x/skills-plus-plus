# Credits

`ask-spp` started as Matt Pocock's [`ask-matt`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/engineering/ask-matt), a router that answers which skill or flow fits the situation by walking the main flow, its on-ramps and the skills that stand alone. `ask-spp` keeps that router shape and points it at spp's own paths and skills.

spp took the idea, not the text: the wording is spp's own.
