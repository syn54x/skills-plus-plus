# Credits

`to-tickets` is a rewrite of Matt Pocock's [`to-tickets`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/engineering/to-tickets). It keeps cutting a spec into vertical slices, agreeing the breakdown with the person before publishing, and filing each slice as an issue with its parent and its blocking edges.

spp took the idea, not the text: the wording is spp's own.
