`wayfinder` is a rewrite of Matt Pocock's [`wayfinder`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/engineering/wayfinder): plan work too large for one session as a map of decision tickets on the issue tracker, resolve them one at a time, and keep the map's ticket types.

spp took the idea, not the text: the wording is spp's own.
