# Credits

`grilling` is a rewrite of Matt Pocock's [`grilling`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/productivity/grilling). It keeps the interview's structure: map the plan as a tree of choices, ask the Frontier (the choices no open choice could reframe) one Round at a time with a recommendation on each, look facts up instead of asking for them, and finish only when the person confirms a shared understanding.

spp took the idea, not the text: the wording is spp's own.
