# Credits

`discuss` draws on three sources.

- Jesse Vincent's Superpowers [`brainstorming`](https://github.com/obra/superpowers/tree/main/skills/brainstorming): one question at a time, asked only when the answer changes direction, which here is the limit of one question to a turn.
- Every's Compound Engineering [`ce-ideate`](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin/tree/main/skills/ce-ideate): a reason for everything that is ruled out, which here is the Ruled out part of the closing restatement.
- OpenSpec's [`openspec-explore`](https://github.com/Fission-AI/OpenSpec/tree/main/skills/openspec-explore): the conversation as the record, with no required artifact, which here is a session that writes no file.

spp took the ideas, not the text: no text was copied, and the wording is spp's own.
