# Credits

`codebase-design` is a rewrite of Matt Pocock's [`codebase-design`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/engineering/codebase-design). It keeps the deep-module vocabulary (deep and shallow modules, interfaces, seams), the advice on designing for testability, and the two reference files on deepening a module and designing it twice.

spp took the idea, not the text: the wording is spp's own.
