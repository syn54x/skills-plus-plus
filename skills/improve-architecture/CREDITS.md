# Credits

`improve-architecture` is a rewrite of Matt Pocock's [`improve-codebase-architecture`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/engineering/improve-codebase-architecture). It keeps the process: explore the codebase for deepening opportunities, present them as a report, and grill the one the person picks before anything is filed.

spp took the idea, not the text: the wording is spp's own.
