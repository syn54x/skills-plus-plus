# Credits

`review` draws on two sources.

- Matt Pocock's [`code-review`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/engineering/code-review): reviewing a change on two separate axes, whether it matches its spec and whether it keeps the repo's standards, reported side by side.
- Jesse Vincent's Superpowers [`subagent-driven-development`](https://github.com/obra/superpowers/tree/main/skills/subagent-driven-development): the reviewer as a fresh subagent that carries none of the author's context, giving a spec verdict and a quality verdict.

spp took the ideas, not the text: the wording is spp's own.
