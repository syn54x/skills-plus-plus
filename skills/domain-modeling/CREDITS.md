# Credits

`domain-modeling` is a rewrite of Matt Pocock's [`domain-modeling`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/engineering/domain-modeling). It keeps the practice of challenging each term against the glossary, sharpening fuzzy language, writing a settled term down at once, offering ADRs sparingly, and the two format files for the glossary and the ADRs.

spp took the idea, not the text: the wording is spp's own.
